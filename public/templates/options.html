Six ways to show the templates

All six are built and working below, using the real template pages. Scroll through, see which one you want, and I will rebuild the gallery around it.

OPTION 01

Device frames

The same page in a laptop and a phone, side by side. Makes the point that every build is responsive without anyone having to say it.

VERDICT

Strong for buyers who worry about mobile. Heavy — two frames per template, so maybe three on the page, not fifteen.

MOBILE · 390px
OPTION 02

Scroll-through

The page creeps down inside the window on a loop, like a screen recording. No cursor, so nothing can fall out of sync. Hovering pauses it.

VERDICT

Nothing to break and it shows the whole page, not just the top. Passive — no sense of someone using it.

pulseclinic.example
HOVER TO PAUSE
OPTION 03

Tabbed switcher

One large window, every template a tab above it. The visitor does the clicking, so they stay in control and the page stays light.

VERDICT

My pick. One frame on the page, fifteen templates reachable, and people engage more when they drive.

meridian.example
OPTION 04

Before / after slider

Drag the handle between a dated site and what it becomes. This is the one that makes someone recognise their own website in the "before".

VERDICT

Most persuasive of the six for cold visitors. Needs a real before — ideally the client's own site.
