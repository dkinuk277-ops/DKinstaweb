How a project runs

Five steps from a demo to your site

The ten here are where conversations start, not where they end. We have built sites that look nothing like any of them — the process is the same either way.

01

Start anywhere

Pick one below, borrow pieces from several, send us a site you admire, or just describe it. These are a vocabulary, not a catalogue — most projects begin as "the Meridian layout, Pulse's booking form, our own colours".

You · 2 minutes
02

Discovery call

Thirty minutes on the phone. What the business does, who you are trying to reach, and what the site actually has to achieve. If your pick is wrong for your audience, we will say so.

Together · Day 1
03

Your design, drafted

We draft your site — your words, your brand, your structure, your page count. Not a recoloured demo. Typically ready three to five days after the call.

Us · Days 2–5
04

Review together

You click through a working preview on a private link, on your own phone, in your own time. Two rounds of changes are included — and "I do not like it" is perfectly good feedback.

Together · Days 5–8
05

Build, launch, look after

We connect your domain, configure email, submit to search engines and hand over a short walkthrough video. Then we keep it hosted, patched and running.

Us · Day 8 onwards
Not seeing your industry? That is normal — these ten cover the sectors we are asked about most, and they are a fraction of what we build. Tell us what you do and we will show you something closer, or start from a blank page.
