About this house

A former rectory of considerable presence, set behind a stone wall on the edge of the village and screened by mature beech. The house has been in the same family since 1971 and comes to market for the first time in over fifty years.

Original sash windows, four working fireplaces and a flagged kitchen that opens onto the south lawn. The coach house to the rear has consent for conversion, granted 2024.

Key features

  • Grade II listed, Georgian origins with Victorian additions
  • Four reception rooms, all south or west facing
  • Coach house with planning consent for a two-bed annexe
  • Walled kitchen garden and original glasshouse
  • Catchment for Prestbury C of E Primary
  • Macclesfield station 2.4 miles — London Euston 1h 48m
