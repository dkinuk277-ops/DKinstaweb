About

We are four people in a room above a bakery in Ancoats. We take on roughly eight projects a year, which is fewer than we could and more than we probably should.

Atelier Nord was founded in 2016 by Ines Moreau and Tom Ashby after a decade between them at larger studios in London and Copenhagen. The intention was simple: work directly with the people making the decisions, and stay small enough that the people who pitch the work are the people who do it.

We work mostly with cultural institutions, independent publishers and founder-led businesses. We are happiest when a project has a constraint worth arguing about.
